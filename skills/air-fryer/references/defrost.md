# Defrosting

The TurboBlaze has no dedicated Defrost button — the manual's function list is Air Fry, Roast, Broil, Frozen, Reheat, Bake, Dehydrate, Proof, Preheat, Warm, and none of them are labeled for thawing. So "defrosting in the air fryer" always means a manual workaround, not a preset — say this up front so the user isn't looking for a button that doesn't exist.

## Food safety context (for your judgment, not to recite every time)

The air fryer isn't a USDA-approved thawing method (those are fridge, cold water, or microwave-then-immediately-cook) — warm uneven heat risks the outside sitting in the 40–140°F danger zone while the inside is still frozen. It's fine for a quick thaw immediately followed by cooking in the same session; it's not fine for thawing something to hold before cooking later. Only surface this to the user if their request implies the "not fine" case (e.g. they say they're thawing for tomorrow) — otherwise just give the setting.

## The two real options

**Option 1 — skip defrosting, cook from frozen (recommended default for anything that fits).** Per the manual: "you can air fry any frozen foods that can be baked in an oven," and the **Frozen** function (390°F default, 300–450°F range) is built for exactly this — nuggets, fries, breaded cutlets, meatballs, pot stickers, thin fish fillets, ground meat patties. Just add several extra minutes versus a thawed version and check doneness with a thermometer for anything raw (chicken 165°F, pork/fish 145°F, ground meat 160°F). This avoids the food-safety gray zone entirely and is almost always faster than defrosting first.

**Option 2 — manual low-temp defrost, for thicker cuts that won't cook through evenly from frozen** (bone-in chicken, thick steaks, roasts): use **Air Fry**, dialed down to **250°F** (within its 300–450°F range if the interface allows, otherwise the lowest available on Air Fry or Bake), 5–8 min per side, checking and flipping frequently. Stop as soon as it's pliable enough to season/cut/arrange — then move straight into the real cook using the appropriate function from [references/cook.md](cook.md) or [references/convert.md](convert.md). Don't let it sit after this step; go straight to cooking.

## What not to try in this appliance

- **Whole roasts, whole chickens, or anything too dense to thaw evenly in under ~15 min** — use the fridge (24 hr) or cold water (renew every 30 min) instead. The air fryer's small basket and direct heat will cook the outside before the inside thaws.
- **Bread dough or anything that needs a slow, even thaw to keep its structure** — use the fridge.
